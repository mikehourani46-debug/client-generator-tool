About Lilly

A Real Estate Pro
Who Truly Cares

With more than three years of behind-the-scenes experience at The Brokerage House, Lilly has developed a deep understanding of the real estate market and the many moving parts that make each transaction successful. Her attention to detail, creative marketing insight, and commitment to excellence have not only shaped her professional growth — they have contributed to the continued growth and success of the brokerage.

Through her work, Lilly has mastered the art of staging, helping clients see beyond four walls and truly connect to the feeling of "home." Her natural ability to elevate spaces, paired with her thoughtful approach to client care, creates a seamless and inspiring experience from start to finish.

With a family background in construction, Lilly brings an added layer of expertise — offering valuable insight into the building process and access to trusted local tradespeople for clients considering improvements or investments.

Lilly is deeply passionate about this community, from supporting local businesses to enjoying everything the beautiful lakes and seasons have to offer. She is dedicated to helping others find not just a house, but a place to truly belong.
